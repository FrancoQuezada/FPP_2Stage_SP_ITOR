In `src/experiments/MethodDispatcher.cpp`, the validator compares the solver's **expected-loss component** to reachability-based expected loss, including when the optimized risk measure is CVaR. ```bash

  --forest-path new_instances/20x20_reburn \
  --results-path new_instances/20x20_reburn \
  --scenario-ids "$REDUCED_TRAIN_IDS_COMMA_SEPARATED" \
